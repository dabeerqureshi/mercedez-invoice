IQ MOTORS INVOICE - HOW TO START
================================

1. Double-click  MercedesInvoice.exe
2. FIRST TIME ONLY: click the "Open Mercedes & Login" button at the top,
   sign in with your Mercedes B2B account (tick "Keep me signed in"),
   then come back to the app.
3. Scan or type part numbers and press Enter. Add vehicle + customer
   details at checkout and click "Save & Email Invoice".

Your saved invoices are in:
    %APPDATA%\MercedesInvoice\Invoices

Your Mercedes login is remembered on this PC - you only sign in once per day
(every other launch opens straight into the catalog).

If Windows SmartScreen shows a warning, click "More info" -> "Run anyway".
